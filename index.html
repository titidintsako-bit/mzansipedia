About MzansiPedia

MzansiPedia is a pseudo social media feed that algorithmically shows you South African content from Simple Wikipedia. It learns what you engage with to suggest more. The algorithm runs locally — no data leaves your device.

Once loaded, it's available fully offline and installable as an app.
