About MzansiPedia

MzansiPedia is a local-first reader for South African Wikipedia topics. It ranks the next card from the categories you choose, posts you open, and posts you like.

Engagement data stays in local storage. The bundled dataset works offline after it loads; live article summaries and images come from Wikimedia when a network is available.

Reader pages add Wikidata facts when Wikipedia returns a matching Wikidata item. Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA; Wikidata facts are CC0.

This project adapts the Xikipedia concept for South African public knowledge.
